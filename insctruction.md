In blood bank, some patients call and ask about storage blood. e.g.: "Hi, I have 2 bags of PCV. Do you have stock of it". If we do, we weclome that patient. Patient pay bill for it.
We take patient sample and stock(pcv in this case) sample from bag. So, same kinds of labels will be printed.
For blood collection, we print the label(patient label in this case) and attach it to that stock blood bag covering the older label. We also provide Compatibility report to the patient as we have that donor record.
