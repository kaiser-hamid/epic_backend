# Blood Bank Module — Project Brief

## 1. Core Workflow

1. **Patient arrival:** The patient presents a bill generated by the existing billing software.
2. **Patient registration:** An executive scans or enters the invoice number and records the required patient details in the Blood Bank module.
3. **Donor registration:** One or more donors are registered and linked to the relevant patient/case.
4. **Sample collection and testing:** The Medical Technologist records sample collection, predefined test results, and observations.
5. **Eligibility recording:** Authorized staff record the donor's eligibility outcome. If a donor is not eligible, another donor can be registered and the applicable new invoice number recorded.
6. **Blood collection:** Once approved under the department's procedures, the collection details are recorded.
7. **Component handling:** The module tracks whole blood and applicable components, such as PCV, FP, CRYO, PLATELET, and PLASMA, using terminology confirmed by the department.
8. **Label and report printing:** The module prints sample labels, blood-bag/component labels, and reports using approved formats.
9. **Storage and monitoring:** Units/components are assigned storage locations and monitored for availability and expiry.
10. **Inventory management:** Staff record the receipt and use of operational consumables and monitor stock levels.

## 5. Core Features

### Patient and Invoice Reference
- Scan or enter the invoice number from the existing billing software.
- Record patient name, age, gender, address, and referral/reference details as required.
- Search records by invoice number and approved patient identifiers.
- Keep the invoice number as a reference; automated verification against the central system requires a confirmed integration method.

### Donor Management
- Register multiple donors against a patient/case.
- Generate a unique donor ID and barcode.
- Record donor screening and eligibility outcomes.
- Preserve previous donor records when another donor is required.
- Restrict sensitive information to authorized staff.

### Sample and Test Records
- Record patient/donor sample collection details.
- Configure the department's predefined tests (currently described as five; exact names and fields need confirmation).
- Record results, observations, timestamps, and responsible staff.
- Support review/approval according to departmental permissions.

### Barcode Labels and Reports
- Print labels for patient and donor samples.
- Print blood-bag/component labels with approved identifiers and required dates.
- Generate reports in the department's predefined format, including patient details, test results, and observations.
- Confirm label sizes, barcode format, printer setup, report fields, and signature requirements before development.

### Blood Collection, Components, and Storage
- Record blood collection and link it to the donor and relevant patient/case.
- Track whole blood and applicable components.
- Record storage location, product/component status, and traceability.
- Search stored units/components by identifier, product type, status, date, or location.
- Track availability, reservation, issue, discard, quarantine, and expiry where applicable.

### Expiry Dashboard
- Show available stock and items approaching expiry.
- Highlight expired or otherwise unavailable units/components.
- Use department-approved expiry rules for each product/component and storage condition.
- Provide filters by product type, status, expiry range, and storage location.

### Consumable Inventory
- Maintain a catalog of operational consumables and selected inventory items.
- Record stock received, issued/used, adjusted, and current balance.
- Configure minimum-stock warnings.
- Track batch/lot numbers and expiry dates where required.
- Keep a history of inventory transactions and adjustments.

### Access Control and Audit Trail
- Define role-based access for executives, Medical Technologists, authorized reviewers, inventory staff, and administrators as needed.
- Record important actions, including result changes, eligibility decisions, collection, stock movement, and report reprints.
- Apply appropriate authentication, backups, data-retention rules, and protection for patient/donor information.

## 6. Scope Boundaries

**Included in the initial scope**
- Blood Bank patient/case registration using an external invoice reference.
- Donor registration and eligibility tracking.
- Sample collection, test result recording, and observations.
- Barcode label and report printing.
- Blood collection, component tracking, storage, and expiry monitoring.
- Blood/component search and consumable inventory.
- User roles and audit history for critical actions.

**Not assumed in the initial scope**
- Replacing the central billing software.
- Generating or modifying bills in the central system.
- Automatic invoice verification without an approved integration.
- Automatic clinical eligibility, compatibility, or blood-release decisions.
- Integration with LIS, accounts, or the existing inventory system unless separately assessed and approved.
- SMS notifications or other external integrations unless agreed as additional scope.

## 7. Requirements to Confirm

Before implementation, confirm:

1. The exact names, fields, and workflow of the predefined tests.
2. Eligibility/deferment statuses and who can approve them.
3. The process for a not-eligible donor and the use of new invoice references.
4. Label dimensions, printer model, barcode format, and required label fields.
5. Report templates, mandatory fields, and signatures.
6. The department's official component terminology and processing workflow.
7. Approved expiry and storage rules for each product/component.
8. Storage-location tracking and whether temperature records are required.
9. Rules for reservation, issue, return, discard, quarantine, and stock correction.
10. Which consumables need stock, batch, and expiry tracking.
11. User roles, permissions, audit requirements, backups, and data retention.
12. Whether the existing billing/LIS/inventory software provides an approved integration method.

## 8. Implementation Approach

1. Confirm the current workflow and departmental rules.
2. Finalize required fields, roles, tests, labels, reports, and inventory items.
3. Implement patient/case registration, invoice references, donor management, and search.
4. Implement sample collection, test recording, eligibility tracking, and reports.
5. Implement blood collection, component tracking, labels, storage, and expiry rules.
6. Implement dashboards, blood/component search, and consumable inventory.
7. Validate workflows and printed outputs with authorized Blood Bank staff before live use.

## 9. Success Criteria

- Staff can find a Blood Bank record using the original invoice number.
- Multiple donors can be linked to the appropriate patient/case.
- Screening, tests, observations, and eligibility outcomes are recorded digitally and traceably.
- Required labels and reports print in approved formats.
- Blood units/components can be traced to their source and storage location.
- Staff can identify available, expiring, expired, and unavailable stock.
- Consumable stock balances and transaction history can be reviewed.
- Critical actions are attributable to a user and timestamp.
